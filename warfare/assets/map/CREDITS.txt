Map data
--------
provinces, borders, capitals, rivers, lakes and cities are derived from Natural Earth (https://www.naturalearthdata.com), public domain:
  ne_10m_admin_0_countries, ne_10m_admin_1_states_provinces, ne_10m_populated_places,
  ne_10m_rivers_lake_centerlines, ne_10m_lakes.
basemap.png, relief.png, seamask.png, shore1.png and shore2.png are generated procedurally by Basemap.java (no third-party imagery).
rivers.bin.gz, lakes.bin.gz and cities.tsv are generated by tools/ExtraLayers.java.
